Privacy Policy

RealWorth app · Last updated 29 September 2026

RealWorth is an app for property practitioners (estate agents) to capture listings and prepare market-related valuation reports. This policy explains what personal information the app and its service collect, why, who it is shared with, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who this applies to

  • Agents who create an account and use the app.
  • Property owners and other contacts whose details an agent captures on a listing. The agent (and their agency) decides what to capture and is responsible for having the owner's permission to do so; RealWorth stores and processes it on the agent's behalf.

3. Who we share it with

  • Service providers that host and store the service for us: our hosting provider (servers and database in [country]) and Cloudflare R2 (photos and documents). Email for password resets is sent through our mail server.
  • Public data services used to look a property up: the City of Cape Town, City of Johannesburg, City of Tshwane and other municipalities, the Chief Surveyor-General, and OpenStreetMap services (Photon and Nominatim) for address search. They receive the address or location being looked up, not who the owner is.
  • Area information services (NASA POWER climate data, census data, and Property24 for homes for sale nearby), which receive a location or suburb only.
  • The app itself loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap and fonts from Google Fonts, which see the phone's IP address.
  • Other agents see sales an agent logs, never who logged them. An agency's agents may see its listings' addresses, sizes and valuations nearby, never the owners.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Reports are created on the agent's phone; the agent decides whom to send them to.

4. How long we keep it

Listings are kept until they are deleted in the app, as the agency's records of its mandates. When an agent deletes their account, their name, contact details, registration numbers, password and profile are deleted straight away; their listings stay with the agency, no longer linked to them, and the sales they logged stay in the shared market data. A property owner may ask for their details on a listing to be removed. Backups are overwritten within [30] days. Server logs are kept for up to [90] days.

5. Security

Information travels encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to the database is restricted to the service and its administrators.

6. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its processing. Agents can edit or delete their listings and profile in the app, and delete their account under Settings → Delete Account, which removes their personal details but leaves their listings with the agency (see how to delete your account). Property owners can ask their agent, or write to privacy@realworth.co.za.

You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za, POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.

7. Children

The app is for property practitioners and is not intended for anyone under 18.
