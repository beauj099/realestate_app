Delete your RealWorth account

RealWorth app

In the app

  1. Open RealWorth and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account, enter your password and confirm.

Without the app

Email privacy@realworth.co.za from the email address you registered with, asking us to delete your account. We will confirm it is you and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, contact details, photo, signature, office details).
  • Every listing you captured, with its owners' details, rooms, photos and documents.
  • The sales you logged.

This happens straight away and cannot be undone. Backups are overwritten within [30] days. An agency you added to the app stays available to other agents, without your name. Reports you already sent to others are not affected.

See also our privacy policy.
