Delete your RealWorth account

RealWorth app

In the app

  1. Open RealWorth and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account, enter your password and confirm.

Without the app

Email privacy@realworth.co.za from the email address you registered with, asking us to delete your account. We will confirm it is you and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address, phone number, registration numbers and password. You can no longer sign in.
  • Your profile: photo, signature, biography, qualifications and office details.

What is kept

  • The listings you captured, with their photos and details. They are your agency's records of its mandates and stay with it, no longer linked to your name. A property owner can ask for their details to be removed (see our privacy policy).
  • Sales you logged stay in the shared market data, which never showed your name.
  • An agency you added to the app stays available to other agents.

Deleting your account happens straight away and cannot be undone. Backups are overwritten within [30] days. Reports you already sent to others are not affected.

See also our privacy policy.
